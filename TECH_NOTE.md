# TBS RoamAgent - Technical Architecture Note

## 1. Executive Summary & Design Philosophy
**TBS RoamAgent** is an agentic AI travel concierge prototype designed for multi-leg intercity bus journeys along the Visakhapatnam to Hyderabad corridor. Built for the Samartha Hackathon challenge ("Agentic AI in Travel Technology"), the system is engineered around **deterministic safety, zero hallucination on hard data, and transparent user consent**.

The core philosophy separates probabilistic language understanding from deterministic constraint satisfaction:
- The **LLM is strictly confined** to natural language intake parsing and fact-checked user explanations.
- The **Deterministic Planner & Independent Validator** hold exclusive authority over schedules, fares, availability, and feasibility.

## 3. Data Architecture & Simulation Model
Every transport record in RoamAgent is explicitly tagged with `source="SIMULATED"` and a fixed freshness timestamp.

### 3.1 Network Topology & Nodes
- **Visakhapatnam Origins**: Vizag Railway Station, MVP Colony, Gajuwaka, Vizag RTC Complex (Dwaraka).
- **Hyderabad Drop Hubs**: MGBS, Miyapur, LB Nagar, Kukatpally.
- **Hyderabad Final Destinations**: Hitech City, Secunderabad, Gachibowli, Ameerpet.

### 3.2 Leg Inventory
1. **First-Mile Legs (Vizag Local)**:
   - Auto and Cab services from city neighborhoods to RTC Complex (20–45 min duration, ₹120–480).
   - Marked `per_vehicle=True`, applying vehicle capacity scaling (`ceil(passengers / 4)`).
2. **Intercity Buses**:
   - 10 overnight buses departing previous evening (18:00–23:30) and arriving morning of travel date.
   - Mix of state operator (APSRTC Garuda Plus, APSRTC Amaravati Multi-Axle) and private carriers (Kaveri Travels, Orange Travels, Morning Star, Dwaraka Express, VRL).
   - Fares: ₹720–1600 per passenger.
   - Varied features: Sleeper/Seater classifications, wheelchair-accessible hydraulic lifts, and seat availability (including edge cases with `seats_left=1`).
3. **Trap Fixture (Bus 10 - SuperSaver Budget Express)**:
   - Departs 22:45, arrives at Miyapur drop point at 09:40 with an ultra-cheap fare of ₹650.
   - Generated by the planner as a candidate, but rejected by the independent validator because arrival exceeds the 09:00 deadline.
4. **Last-Mile Legs (Hyderabad Local)**:
   - **Hyderabad Metro Rail**: 06:00 to 22:00 at 10-minute intervals. Accessible, ₹35–65 per person.
   - **Local Cabs & Autos**: Available across all drop points and destinations.

## 4. Planner, Validator & Risk Ranking Mathematics

### 4.1 Multi-Leg Chaining & Buffer Rules
The planner enumerates candidate chains: `[bus]`, `[first -> bus]`, `[bus -> last]`, and `[first -> bus -> last]`.
- Continuity condition: `leg[i+1].from_node == leg[i].to_node`.
- Minimum buffer condition:
  $$\text{buffer} = \text{dep}_{i+1} - \text{arr}_i \ge \begin{cases} 15 \text{ mins} & \text{if either leg is a bus} \\ 10 \text{ mins} & \text{if local-to-local} \end{cases}$$
- Total fare formula:
  $$\text{Total Cost} = \sum_{l \in \text{person legs}} (\text{fare}_l \times \text{pax}) + \sum_{l \in \text{vehicle legs}} \left(\text{fare}_l \times \lceil \text{pax} / 4 \rceil \right)$$

### 4.2 Independent Validator
The validator runs completely independently from the candidate generator. It verifies:
1. $\text{Arrival Time} \le \text{Arrival Deadline}$
2. $\text{Total Fare} \le \text{Budget Total INR}$ (if budget is set)
3. Transfer buffers $\ge$ minimum physical thresholds
4. $\text{Seat Type} = \text{sleeper}$ (if sleeper requested)
5. Bus $\text{seats\_left} \ge \text{passengers}$
6. Wheelchair accessibility across 100% of legs in the chain (if requested)
7. All leg statuses $\in \{\text{"ok"}, \text{"delayed"}\}$ (no cancelled or unavailable legs)

### 4.3 Risk Evaluation Formula
RoamAgent assesses transfer slack relative to typical operator delay:
$$\text{Risk Ratio} = \frac{\text{Transfer Buffer (mins)}}{\max(1, \text{Typical Delay of Incoming Leg (mins)})}$$
For direct bus routes:
$$\text{Risk Ratio} = \frac{\text{Deadline} - \text{Arrival}}{\max(1, \text{Typical Delay of Bus})}$$
- **Low Risk ($\ge 2.0$)**: Buffer is at least double the typical delay.
- **Medium Risk ($1.0 - 2.0$)**: Buffer matches expected variance.
- **High Risk ($< 1.0$)**: Tight buffer; incoming delay could break connection.

### 4.4 Balanced Ranking Score
$$\text{Score} = 0.4 \cdot \text{norm}(\text{Cost}) + 0.3 \cdot \text{norm}(\text{Duration}) + 0.3 \cdot \text{norm}(\text{Risk Score})$$
Where lower is better, with deterministic tie-breaking on `itinerary.id`.

## 5. Disruption Engine & Re-Planning Mechanics
The disruption engine operates as a pure state mutator:
1. `apply_disruptions(base_legs, events)` returns a brand-new leg graph.
   - `DELAY`: Shifts `dep` and `arr` timestamps forward by `minutes`.
   - `CANCEL`: Sets leg status to `cancelled`.
   - `MISSED_CONNECTION`: Sets leg status to `unavailable` and sets re-plan reference time to current timestamp.
   - `LOCAL_UNAVAILABLE`: Marks specific local feeder as `unavailable`.
2. The planner recomputes options on the mutated graph, filtering out past legs and the currently booked itinerary.
3. Alternatives are annotated with:
   - `cost_delta = alt.total_cost - booked.total_cost`
   - `arrival_delta_min = alt.arrival - booked.arrival`
   - `risk_change = booked.risk_label -> alt.risk_label`
4. If zero feasible options remain, RoamAgent returns the closest near-miss candidate, specifies the violated constraint, suggests parameter relaxations, and outputs the partner disclaimer.

## 6. Safety Consent Gate & Sandbox Payments
To prevent autonomous financial or booking operations:
- **Autonomous Operations**: `search`, `compare`, `recommend`, `notify`, `explain`.
- **Gated Operations (Strict Approval Required)**: `book`, `pay`, `cancel`, `rebook`.
- The `/book` endpoint strictly checks `approved == true`. If missing or false, it immediately halts with **HTTP 403 Forbidden**.
- **Price Tampering Protection**: Client-sent fares are ignored. The server re-runs the deterministic planner over the verified state, computes the true total, and issues a sandbox payment token (`SBX-<8 hex>`).

## 7. Mock vs. Real Deployment Gap Analysis

| Capability | RoamAgent Prototype | Production Deployment Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Operator Data** | Deterministic static graph | Real-time GDS APIs (APSRTC OPRS, AbhiBus, RedBus API) with live GPS vehicle tracking |
| **Booking Authority** | Server simulated reservation | Licensed OTA / Aggregator API credentials with PNR generation and seat-lock webhooks |
| **Payment Gateway** | Sandbox token (`SBX-XXXX`) | PCI-DSS compliant PSP (Razorpay, Cashfree, UPI Intent) with 2FA and webhook reconciliation |
| **Notifications** | Frontend toast & drawer feed | WhatsApp Business API, SMS Gateway (Twilio/Gupshup), and mobile Push Notifications |
| **Consent & Audit** | Memory state gate with 403 checks | Cryptographic consent log, audit trail database, and session auth tokens |
| **Data Retention** | Session memory only (ephemeral) | DPDP Act (India) compliant encrypted storage with automated data expiration policies |

## 8. Limitations & Regulated-Services Disclaimer
- **Browser Speech Support**: Web Speech API is dependent on browser implementation (native on Chromium-based browsers; hidden automatically when unsupported).
- **Telugu Localization**: Telugu output strings are generated from templated translations and labeled experimental.
- **Regulatory Disclaimer**: *TBS RoamAgent is a research prototype developed exclusively for the Samartha Hackathon. It does not process real monetary transactions, issue actual bus tickets, or provide insurance/compensation guarantees.*

## 9. Business Path (Hypotheses to Validate)
Future commercial feasibility rests on validating three core hypotheses:
1. **Value of Multi-Leg Guarantees**: Will intercity travelers pay a modest convenience fee (e.g., ₹29–49) for guaranteed first-to-last-mile transfer buffers and proactive replanning?
2. **Disruption Anxiety Reduction**: Does real-time automated replanning decrease customer support call volume by over 40% during highway delays?
3. **Operator Collaboration**: Will private bus operators expose webhook endpoints for automated seat re-allocation during vehicle breakdowns?
